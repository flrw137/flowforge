## What we believe (philosophy)

- Heading: `Precision over decoration.`
- Body: `Interfaces should communicate through typography, spacing, and composition — not effects. Systems should be as considered under the hood as they are on the surface. If a visual element doesn't earn its place, we remove it.`

---

## How we work (approach)

- Heading: `A smaller number of projects, done properly.`
- Body: `We work in short cycles with working software at the end of each one. You talk directly to the people building. Decisions are made where the work happens, not in a chain of intermediaries.`

---

## Process

- Heading: `Four phases, no mystery.`
- Steps:
  1. `Discover` — `We understand the problem, the constraints, and what success actually looks like.`
  2. `Design` — `We shape the solution: architecture, interface, and the trade-offs in between.`
  3. `Build` — `We ship working increments weekly. You see progress, not decks.`
  4. `Support` — `We stay after launch for the part most studios skip: making sure it keeps working.`
